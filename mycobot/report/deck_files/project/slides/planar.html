Planar 2-DOF companion

The pattern is not specific to the UR5e

8 of 9 design rankings agree with the UR5e. The exception is the offset residual.

About 2× success for a residual on torque control with a ±20% mass error: the one setting where it beats its base.

It gives up most of the exact-plant performance to do so.

The planar arm is cheap to train, so it was used to test design choices before the expensive UR5e runs, and then to check that the UR5e result is not an artefact of one arm.
