Why it fails

A residual cannot help a planner whose path is already tracked

Bounded, so cancelled. The additive residual can move the target by at most 0.012 rad per joint, and the tracker pulls it back.

Unbounded, so unsafe. The offset residual has authority, but it saturates and leaves the safe path.

The authority and safety dilemma. The bound is derived in Appendix A and measured on the 87% of unsaturated steps: 0.0120 rad.
