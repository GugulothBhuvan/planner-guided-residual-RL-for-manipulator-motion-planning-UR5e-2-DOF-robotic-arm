The fix

Send a few checked segments and let the firmware plan each one

1

Play it in the model

The usual controller runs the episode on the simulated arm and gives the joint path.

2

Thin and verify

Keep a few waypoints (6° tolerance). Check every segment against the table, the arm and the virtual obstacles, including a late-starting wrist.

3

Send and watch

One firmware move per waypoint at speed 40. Stop on a deviation, a joint limit or a virtual contact.

A protocol change: the residual's effect is replayed from the simulation, not corrected tick by tick from the arm's measurements.

Firmware behaviour found on the way: a move from standstill is a straight line in joint space to within about 1.5 degrees; sending the next waypoint while moving makes the wrist start 0.3 to 0.5 seconds late and its readings freeze, so the arm settles at every waypoint.
