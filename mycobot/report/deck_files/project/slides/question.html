The question

Can a cheap planner plus a learned correction match an expensive planner?

Expensive planner

Full RRT-Connect finds a collision-free path in 99% of cluttered scenes, but spends a large collision-check budget.

Cheap planner

Limited to 1,500, 3,000 or 6,000 collision checks. Fast, but it fails more often.

Learned residual

A reinforcement-learning policy (SAC) corrects the cheap planner's path. Can it close the gap?

The research question in one line. The answer in this project is no for every setting tested, and the measurements say why.
