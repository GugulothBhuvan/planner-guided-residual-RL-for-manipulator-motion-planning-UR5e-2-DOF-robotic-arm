# Summary of the R1/R2 reach attempt, for the report (a limitation, not a result)

To check the learned approach on the MyCobot in a simpler setting than the residual, a goal-conditioned reach policy was trained in simulation with DDPG and hindsight experience replay (position goal in a 10 cm cube, 2 cm tolerance, sparse reward, tool-space actions converted to joint steps of at most 0.5 deg per tick). With a fixed start and no obstacle (R1), three seeds trained for 100,000 steps reached 27%, 6% and 68% of 100 test episodes, with no contact, against 100% for a scripted straight line to the goal; the learned controller did not reach the 90% bar within that budget, and the runs were stopped there. With one sphere placed on the straight line (R2), no detour in tool space could clear it: debug runs showed that the wrist links and the tool body trail the tool tip by a few centimetres and touch a sphere that the tip clears by 7 cm, and moving the sphere along the line, making it smaller, moving the goal cube outward and keeping 4 cm from the arm at the start left at most 5% of sampled episodes solvable by any hand-coded detour (0% at the planned placement). The three R2 policies trained as planned ended at 0% success with 55-88% contact on validation. These runs are reported as a limitation of the attempt (a short training budget and a task geometry that the tool-space action cannot satisfy on this arm), not as a result about the method, and nothing from them is used elsewhere in this report. No reach policy was run on the real arm.
